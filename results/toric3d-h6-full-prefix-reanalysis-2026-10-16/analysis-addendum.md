# Secondary X crossing assessment

The independent check of the planned simultaneous adjacent-size bands found
that every adjacent pair remains compatible with zero difference throughout
the measured window. The planned primary result is retained and explicitly
called uninformative for localization.

Add a secondary intersection over **all ten size pairs**, rather than choosing
one favorable contrast. This uses exactly the same 35 simultaneously covered
binomial probabilities. Every pairwise difference bound follows on that same
joint coverage event, so no additional multiplicity adjustment is needed for
the derived contrasts. The piecewise-linear interpolation assumption still
applies; neither intersection proves uniqueness or an asymptotic threshold.

This extension was chosen after examining the adjacent-size bands. It is
reported as an exploratory extension, not a previously registered analysis.
No simulation, point, fit window, sample count, or original result is changed.
